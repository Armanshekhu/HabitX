How rewards work

Rewards

+10 XP
Check in
Complete a habit for today. Unchecking it takes the XP back, so it can't be farmed.
+20 XP
Perfect day
Complete every habit scheduled for today. Awarded once per day.
100 XP
One level
Level = total XP ÷ 100, rounded down, plus 1.
Streak
Scheduled days only
Days a habit isn't scheduled never break the streak.
Badges
Earned once
First Check-in, 3-Day Streak, 7-Day Streak, Perfect Day, 50 Check-ins, Level 5.
